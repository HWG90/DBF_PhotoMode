# 🪿 WAR JOURNALIST 1.0
### A Helldivers 2 Photo Mode
**WE MADE IT! A HUGE THANK YOU to everyone submitting photos, testing builds and reporting bugs.** Your shots have been amazing, and your feedback helped get us here. Please keep them coming! 📸

- **Cinema action shots:** place the camera, then control your Helldiver. Return to reposition; Freeze comes back if enabled, and slow motion is preserved. The Cinema hint fades.
- **Camera paths:** up to **32 points**, segment timing, smooth sweeps or cuts while you perform.
- **Global controls:** Freeze, Slow Motion/speed, Lock Camera and solo **Normal / Passive / Dummy AI**, with faction icons and Reset.
- **Fixes:** frozen ragdoll spinning now holds the captured pose and resumes normally; the extra cape variant follows; Maxigun belts hide/restore. Native-menu/chat handoffs, gameplay camera recovery and double-click focus are corrected. Held ADS stays intact.
- **Saved settings:** menu placement/opacity and controls-drawer visibility/layout survive restarts—including turning the legend off.

213 sampled pose frames/variants, Native DoF, shader bokeh preview, autofocus, lights/presets, effects and artwork are bundled. Supported **ActionCam 2.3.1/2.4** can stay enabled. No Link addon required.

**Choose one runtime. Keep settings/presets/screenshots, replace the old package, deploy matching assets and restart.**
