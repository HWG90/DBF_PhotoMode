# 🪿 WAR JOURNALIST 0.9.7

We have been COOKING. This one brings everything since 0.9.5 together.

**The new toys**
- **213 fixed pose frames.** Gestures, cooperative variants, kneeling poses and both push-ups, without unpausing. Reset puts you back. Sampled frames, not 213 store emotes.
- **Solo Director AI:** Normal, Passive and Dummy, with Reset. Behavior persists after Photo Mode closes and covers new enemies. Disabled in multiplayer.
- **Drag your lights into place.** Numbered indicators, Shift precision, subject aiming, three lighting rigs, and **five lighting setups saved across restarts**.
- **Stronger shader DoF:** 1x–8x blur strength and more samples. Native is still the quality baseline.
- **Menu opacity** in the bottom-right corner, saved across restarts.
- **Color editor Apply button**, live swatch and HEX preview.

**And the bug fixes**
- **Floating cape: FIXED for supported capes.** It follows your shoulders and restores correctly. The second image shows the old bug before the fix.
- Poses no longer stay grayed out because the game clock is paused.
- Lights return when you reopen Photo Mode in the same scene.
- Hidden subjects no longer leave nested equipment floating behind.
- Fixed enemies staying hostile in Dummy/Passive; both modes re-tested live.
