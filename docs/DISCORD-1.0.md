# 🪿 WAR JOURNALIST 1.0
## A Helldivers 2 Photo Mode

**WE MADE IT. 1.0 is here!**

**A HUGE THANK YOU to everyone sharing photos, testing builds and helping chase down bugs.** Seeing what you're making with this thing has been amazing. Your shots and feedback helped get us here. Please keep them coming. 📸

- **Action shots:** place the camera, hit **Begin action shot**, and control your Helldiver while the camera stays put. Press your Photo Mode key to reposition. Freeze returns if you had it enabled; your slow-motion speed is kept.
- **Camera paths:** record up to **32 points**, set segment durations, and use **Smooth** movement or **Cuts** while you perform. Paths stay with the current scene/session.
- **Global controls:** Freeze Action, Slow Motion + speed, Lock Camera and **Normal / Passive / Dummy AI** stay within reach. AI includes a faction icon, persists outside Photo Mode, affects new enemies and has Reset. **Solo only.**
- **Input cleanup:** Escape/settings and chat get priority, gameplay camera/cursor handoffs recover correctly, camera-mode confinement is restored, and buffered double-click focus catches fast clicks. The Helldivers cursor is back too.
- **Pose fixes:** the reported cape variant now follows correctly. The spinning ragdoll at zero time now holds its captured pose and resumes normally in the tested reproduction. The separate **Maxigun ammo belt hides with its equipment**.
- **Everything stays bundled:** 213 sampled pose frames/variants, Native DoF, shader bokeh preview, autofocus, lights/presets, scene effects, logos, saved Director cameras and the movable menu/context drawer.

Supported **ActionCam 2.3.1 / 2.4 can stay enabled**. No Link addon or separate Photo Mode asset download required.

**Replace the old Photo Mode package, deploy its matching bundled assets and restart. Keep your settings, presets and screenshots. Choose one runtime.**
