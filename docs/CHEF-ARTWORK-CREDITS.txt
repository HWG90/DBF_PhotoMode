War Journalist Chef preview artwork

The Chef build converts the existing War Journalist monochrome geometry into
bounded rectangle spans. This reduces fine edge detail. The Helldiver crest
also retains its triangle representation. Original sources are unchanged in
the source repository; this candidate contains derived numeric geometry.
