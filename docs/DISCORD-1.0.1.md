### A Helldivers 2 Photo Mode
**Actually 1.0 this time.**

**THANK YOU to everyone sharing photos, testing builds and reporting bugs.** Your shots have been amazing. Please keep them coming! 📸

- **Slim or Stacked tabs:** choose under **Composition → Menu layout → Tab layout**. Saved through restarts; global controls stay reachable.
- **Cute new icons:** Director clapperboard, Subject helmet, Controls cross.

BSL startup and both layouts were confirmed in-game. MDL/LLL package startup checks passed. The accepted compositor is unchanged.

**All the big 1.0 tools stay:** static Cinema action shots, 32-point camera paths with smooth travel/cuts, Freeze/Slow Motion/Lock globals, solo Normal/Passive/Dummy AI, 213 sampled pose frames, fixed ragdoll/capes, Maxigun belt hiding, Native DoF, shader bokeh preview, autofocus, lights, effects and saved Director cameras.
