# War Journalist 0.9.11 development candidate

Place the Photo Mode camera, then choose **Director → Begin action shot**. The camera stays at its world-space position, angle, roll and field of view while gameplay controls return to the Helldiver. The scene resumes after owned fixed poses and pause overrides are restored. Photo effects and lighting remain attached to the placed camera.

Press your usual Photo Mode key to reposition the camera. The screenshot shortcut remains available during the action shot. Exit Photo Mode returns to the gameplay camera. Cinema state is temporary and never activates automatically on restart.

This candidate preserves 0.9.9 assets and the AMD registry repair. Includes the 0.9.10 compatibility adapter: supported ActionCam 2.3.1 yields its camera throughout placement and Cinema performance, then resumes after all Photo cleanup succeeds. Both load orders and late loading are covered by isolated fixtures. Static shot, menu handoff, aiming/projectile direction and combat need live acceptance; no live validation or publication is claimed.
