0.9.9 corrects an incomplete bundled shader registry. The tested 0.9.8 runtime, features, UI and artwork are retained.

[size=4][color=#F4CA35][b]0.9.9 COMPATIBILITY HOTFIX[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*]Removed 198 inherited shader registrations whose libraries were absent from a clean Photo Mode package. The registry now contains 77 native registrations plus the 44 shipped Photo Mode shader libraries: 121 total.
[*]Added package checks for missing, duplicate and unbundled registrations, while preserving the native registration order.
[/list]
Affected Radeon testers reported stable Alt-Tab, fullscreen, bloom and DoF changes with the registry-only candidate, both before and after opening Photo Mode. This repairs the tested compatibility issue; without an AMD crash dump, it does not establish a fix for every display/driver crash. HDR/SDR switching and unrelated crashes still need separate confirmation.

0.9.8 fixes Native DoF flicker and preserves held ADS/gameplay inputs when entering and leaving Photo Mode. Native blur stayed steady in the live test, and held ADS now stays correct while posing. All 0.9.7 features, artwork and UI are retained.

[size=4][color=#F4CA35][b]0.9.8 HOTFIX[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*]Native DoF no longer resets its valid lens profile during brief empty or stock camera samples. Real camera changes still use ownership and validity guards.
[*]Removed forced keyboard/mouse releases around Photo Mode. Held ADS is preserved; camera mouse capture and the guarded hoverpack pause remain.
[/list]
Both corrections were verified in-game. All 54 fixture groups and standalone BSL, MDL and LLL package checks passed. Broader camera transitions and hoverpack/controller combinations still need testing.

[size=4][color=#F4CA35][b]A PROPER LENS
[/b][/color][/size][img]https://staticdelivery.nexusmods.com/mods/6119/images/17248/17248-1791893648-484331057.jpg[/img]
[b]Native camera DoF[/b] now exposes focus distance and aperture. [b]Double-click a subject[/b] to focus repeatedly, or enable [b]Center auto-focus[/b] to track the center of the frame. There are 24/35/50/85/135 mm lens presets, roll/portrait shortcuts, and fine focus steps down to 0.001 m.

[b]Shader DoF[/b] offers stronger round or hexagonal bokeh: 1–8× blur strength (4× by default), with 64 samples in Standard and 128 in Smoother. This new strength/quality path remains a preview and needs broader visual testing. Its full-resolution compositor now allows scene effects and color grading alongside DoF. The former dim/cool tint and focused-color rectangle were corrected. Native remains the blur-quality baseline; Shader remains a preview.

[size=4][color=#F4CA35][b]STRIKE A POSE[/b][/color][/size]
[img]https://ayakamods.com/attachments/20261013151354_1-jpg.39242/[/img]
[b]213 sampled pose frames and variants[/b] are available under Subject, including Salute, Wave, Taunt, push-ups and the dab shown here. Turn [b]Pause entire action[/b] ON and choose a pose: your Helldiver changes pose immediately while the world stays frozen. [b]Reset pose[/b] restores the original stance. These are sampled emote/action frames, not a claim of 213 unique store emotes; unidentified clips retain development labels. Props and a second participant are not spawned.

Supported capes follow the posed shoulders with their original appearance intact, including across pose changes and Reset. The existing frozen cloth shape is retained; cloth is not re-simulated.

[size=4][color=#F4CA35][b]LIGHT THE SCENE[/b][/color][/size]
[size=4][color=#F4CA35][b][img]https://staticdelivery.nexusmods.com/mods/6119/images/17248/17248-1791892833-1132135075.png[/img][/b][/color][/size]
Add up to three owned point or spot lights. Adjust color, intensity up to 10,000, range up to 1,000 m, cone, placement and camera follow. On-screen indicators show their position; remove individual lights or clear them all. Reopening Photo Mode recreates your placed lights in the same scene; you no longer have to add them again. Numbered, color-coded indicators stay visible with the menu open and can be dragged across the camera plane. Hold Shift for finer placement. The color editor has an Apply button and a live swatch/HEX preview.

Choose Key / Fill / Rim, Portrait, or Dramatic side lighting presets aimed at the selected subject. Save up to five subject-relative lighting setups across restarts. New lighting tools and broader mission coverage: [b]Not tested fully.[/b]

[b]Dedicated mission light asset included.[/b] This release includes a dedicated light unit so spawning no longer relies on a ship-only prefab. The earlier ship light worked in-game; the new mission asset has offline validation and still needs broader live testing.

[size=4][color=#F4CA35][b]DIRECT THE ACTION[/b][/color][/size]
Director now offers [b]Normal, Passive and Dummy[/b] enemy behavior in verified solo missions. Passive suppresses detection while allowing movement; Dummy also restricts ordinary movement. The selection continues after leaving Photo Mode and is applied to newly discovered enemies. Use [b]Reset AI to Normal[/b] to restore combat. Joining players reset the behavior and gray these controls out; restarting the game begins at Normal. Physics, damage and an attack already in progress can still continue.
