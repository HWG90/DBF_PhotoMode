[size=3][color=#E6E2D4]Muzzle flashes, flying brass, a cape caught mid-turn. Stop the action in solo play and find the angle you never get during a firefight.[/color][/size]
War Journalist gives you a free camera and a photo menu for composing the shot. Move around the scene, adjust roll and field of view, save a camera position, or use slow motion to set up the next frame.

[color=#DDB869][b]Preview limits:[/b][/color] Focus distance, aperture/f-stop and scene color/depth shaders are disabled in this release. Full details are below.

[size=4][color=#F4CA35][b]CAMERA & COMPOSITION[/b][/color][/size]
[color=#9BB6BF][b]Position the camera[/b][/color]
Free movement, mousewheel speed, Q/E roll, vertical field of view and camera lock. [b]Save Camera Position[/b] keeps a shot while you remain within its configurable [b]5–60 m[/b] radius. Leaving that radius, returning to the player, changing scenes, dying or restarting clears it.

[color=#9BB6BF][b]Shape the frame[/b][/color]
Tint filters, decorative shader patterns, vignette, grain, framing guides and logos. The menu resizes with its text and controls. Type numeric values directly, or right-click an enabled setting to restore its default.

[color=#9BB6BF][b]Stop the action in solo play[/b][/color]
Solo pause stops native simulation, player animation and AI. Pause and slow motion require a verified solo session; other players, joining peers or unknown session state prevent activation. Freecam remains available in multiplayer. Losing window focus keeps the solo pause active.

[size=4][color=#F4CA35][b]DEFAULT CONTROLS[/b][/color][/size]
[b]P[/b] — enter or exit Photo Mode
[b]WASD[/b] — move the camera
[b]Shift / Ctrl[/b] — rise / descend
[b]Mousewheel[/b] — adjust movement speed
[b]Q / E[/b] — roll the camera; switch tabs in menu focus
[b]Tab[/b] — switch camera / menu focus
[b]L[/b] — lock or unlock the camera
[b]Backslash (\)[/b] — prototype solo slow motion
[b]H[/b] — hide photo controls
[b]Enter / Space[/b] — choose or edit; WASD moves the menu selector
[b]Escape[/b] — cancel or leave
All bindings are configurable.

[size=4][color=#F4CA35][b]SCREENSHOTS[/b][/color][/size]
The [b]Screenshot[/b] button temporarily hides photo controls, requests game HUD hiding, and restores your visibility settings afterward. Built-in capture saves PNG files.
[b]BSL:[/b] %LOCALAPPDATA%/DBF/PhotoMode/Screenshots
[b]MDL:[/b] the mod folder’s Screenshots directory
Steam screenshots remain an alternative.

[size=4][color=#F4CA35][b]KNOWN ISSUES • 0.5 PREVIEW[/b][/color][/size]
[color=#DDB869][b]Unavailable controls[/b][/color]
Focus distance and aperture/f-stop have no working implementation here; both sliders are disabled. Scene color/depth shaders are disabled and not included. Exposure, saturation and contrast controls are also unavailable.
Native bloom/DoF toggles use the game’s existing graphics. They do not provide custom focus or aperture.

[color=#DDB869][b]Capture & overlay limits[/b][/color]
Built-in PNG capture may retain ship UI, and HDR brightness can differ from the displayed image. Decorative shader overlays require DBF-HUD assets; missing materials are skipped.
