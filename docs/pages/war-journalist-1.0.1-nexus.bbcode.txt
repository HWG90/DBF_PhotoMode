[b]Native camera DoF[/b] now exposes focus distance and aperture. [b]Double-click a subject[/b] to focus repeatedly, or enable [b]Center auto-focus[/b] to track the center of the frame. There are 24/35/50/85/135 mm lens presets, roll/portrait shortcuts, and fine focus steps down to 0.001 m.

[b]Shader DoF[/b] offers stronger round or hexagonal bokeh: 1–8× blur strength (4× by default), with 64 samples in Standard and 128 in Smoother. This new strength/quality path remains a preview and needs broader visual testing. Its full-resolution compositor now allows scene effects and color grading alongside DoF. The former dim/cool tint and focused-color rectangle were corrected. Native remains the blur-quality baseline; Shader remains a preview.

[size=4][color=#F4CA35][b]STRIKE A POSE[/b][/color][/size]
[img]https://staticdelivery.nexusmods.com/mods/6119/images/17248/17248-1791919266-1050282945.jpg[/img]
[b]213 sampled pose frames and variants[/b] are available under Subject, including Salute, Wave, Taunt, push-ups and the dab shown here. Turn [b]Freeze Action[/b] ON and choose a pose: your Helldiver changes pose immediately while the world stays frozen. [b]Reset pose[/b] restores the original stance. These are sampled emote/action frames, not a claim of 213 unique store emotes; unidentified clips retain development labels. Props and a second participant are not spawned.

Supported capes follow the posed shoulders with their original appearance intact, including across pose changes and Reset. The additional outfit/cape variant reported by testers is corrected too. [size=4][color=#F4CA35][b]LIGHT THE SCENE[/b][/color][/size]
[size=4][color=#F4CA35][b][img]https://staticdelivery.nexusmods.com/mods/6119/images/17248/17248-1791892833-1132135075.png[/img][/b][/color][/size]
Add up to three owned point or spot lights. Adjust color, intensity up to 10,000, range up to 1,000 m, cone, placement and camera follow. On-screen indicators show their position; remove individual lights or clear them all. Reopening Photo Mode recreates your placed lights in the same scene; you no longer have to add them again. Numbered, color-coded indicators stay visible with the menu open and can be dragged across the camera plane. Hold Shift for finer placement. The color editor has an Apply button and a live swatch/HEX preview.

Choose Key / Fill / Rim, Portrait, or Dramatic side lighting presets aimed at the selected subject. Save up to five subject-relative lighting setups across restarts. New lighting tools and broader mission coverage: [b]Not tested fully.[/b]

[b]Dedicated mission light asset included.[/b] This release includes a dedicated light unit so spawning no longer relies on a ship-only prefab. The earlier ship light worked in-game; the new mission asset has offline validation and still needs broader live testing.

[size=4][color=#F4CA35][b]DIRECT THE ACTION[/b][/color][/size]
The global AI toolbar offers [b]Normal, Passive and Dummy[/b] enemy behavior in verified solo missions. Passive suppresses detection while allowing movement; Dummy also restricts ordinary movement. The selection continues after leaving Photo Mode and is applied to newly discovered enemies. Use [b]Reset AI to Normal[/b] to restore combat. Joining players reset the behavior and gray these controls out; restarting the game begins at Normal. Physics, damage and an attack already in progress can still continue. The control includes an icon for the current mission's enemy faction. AI processing itself remains active.

[b]Action shots and camera paths[/b]
Place the camera, then choose [b]Director → Begin action shot[/b]. Your Helldiver regains gameplay controls while the photo camera stays where you put it. Use your screenshot shortcut, then press your Photo Mode key to reposition; Exit Photo Mode returns to the gameplay camera. If Freeze was enabled before the shot, returning to placement freezes again. Your selected slow-motion rate is preserved throughout.

Add up to [b]32 camera points[/b], set each segment's duration, and choose [b]Smooth[/b] or [b]Cut[/b]. Play the path while you perform; its final view stays held. These paths are temporary and belong to the current player/scene. A brief unavailable scene read no longer discards recorded points, while confirmed player or scene changes end the shot and clear them. Five saved player-relative Director positions remain a separate feature and survive restarts.

[b]BSL — recommended main file[/b]
[url=https://www.nexusmods.com/helldivers2/mods/17248?tab=files]Download War-Journalist-1.0.1-BSL.zip[/url]
Requires Bingus Shared Loader v15+ / API 1. Import the complete BSL ZIP into HD2 Arsenal, enable it alongside BSL, deploy and restart normally. Manual patch installation is covered in INSTALL.txt.

[b]MDL — optional alternative[/b]
[url=https://www.nexusmods.com/helldivers2/mods/17248?tab=files]Download War-Journalist-1.0.1-MDL.zip[/url]
Requires MDL API 2. Copy the included dbf_freecam folder into MDL Mods. Import this complete MDL ZIP into Arsenal to install its GameData assets, enable and deploy, then restart. The package contains no nested archives. GameData is also available for manual installation; use one asset installation method. Arsenal installs the assets only; the MDL runtime folder must still be copied separately.

[b]Nexus provides BSL and MDL. [url=https://github.com/HWG90/DBF_PhotoMode/releases/tag/v1.0.1]GitHub provides BSL, MDL and LLL[/url]. Choose one runtime.[/b] Every ZIP includes all required runtime, shader, material, light and logo resources. No separate asset download is required. MCM, DBF-HUD, ActionCam and Link's Link to Memory are not prerequisites. MCM integration is optional.

When upgrading, preserve settings/presets/screenshots, disable the older Photo Mode runtime, and replace any older separate Photo asset candidate with the matching bundled assets. Restart after deploying assets.

[b]Supported ActionCam 2.3.1 and 2.4 can remain enabled.[/b] ActionCam handles normal gameplay; War Journalist takes the photo/cinema camera and returns ownership after cleanup. Unknown ActionCam versions and other camera mods need separate compatibility work.
