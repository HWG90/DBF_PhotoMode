# Known issues in 0.9.11 development candidate

- **Cinema camera is an offline-tested development candidate.** Static pose/input handoff is covered by fixtures; live aiming/projectile direction, hoverpack, native menu/display handoffs and ActionCam coexistence need live acceptance. Cinema mode never starts automatically after restart, and player/scene changes end the mode.
- **Photo lighting: Not tested fully across scenes.** Dedicated bundled light assets have worked in the running game; availability still requires those assets to be loaded after restart. Coverage of every mission and transition is incomplete.
- Native focus/aperture and camera-instance recovery were user-tested during development. The 0.9.8 correction was confirmed steady while still and moving after a temporary render-queue timeout had caused flicker. This does not prove every scene, game update, ragdoll or transition. Controls wait for a validated current camera rather than writing through a stale one.
- Shader DoF and scene grading share a full-resolution compositor. HDR tint/exposure was accepted with neutral grading, no scene effect and active Shader DoF. SDR compositor parity and every effect combination remain unverified. Blurred HDR energy above GUI white remains limited by the GUI carrier. Native remains the blur-quality baseline.
- WARMING UP SCENE may remain briefly after launch or display changes while resources stabilize. Install all assets included with your chosen loader package and restart after changing them.
- The reported hoverpack + melee pause/resume reproduction passed with the data-only guard before this hotfix. Entering and leaving Photo Mode now preserves held gameplay inputs, including ADS, instead of forcing synthetic releases. Active-hover guarded pause still requires neutral hover controls and may refuse entry while movement/jump is held; this combination has not received a new live regression test. Connected or unidentified controllers refuse active-hover guarded pause. Pause and slow motion require a verified solo session.
- Screenshot conversion includes SDR channel and HDR brightness fixes; ship UI may still appear. The rebindable Pause shortcut is fixture-tested; this release does not claim a separate live keyboard-trigger validation or intercept Windows Print Screen.
- Director offers Normal, Passive and Dummy behavior with Reset. Persistence after closing Photo Mode and restoration of combat were user-tested. The controls require verified solo mission authority; multiplayer hosts and clients are disabled. Enemy brains, damage and physics continue running. Frozen/stun mode is not included.
- Stronger shader DoF, draggable light indicators and saved subject-focused setups still need fresh live acceptance. Fixed Salute was visually confirmed while world rate/delta stayed at zero, followed by user confirmation of pose switching, both push-ups and Reset. Mission and respawn/ragdoll transitions need broader live coverage. Fixed poses require Pause entire action. Catalogue variants are sampled action frames, not verified individual released emotes; unidentified clips keep development labels. Props and other participants are not spawned.
- Supported capes now receive an original-garment placement correction when selecting a fixed pose. The cape follows the posed shoulder anchor while retaining its frozen cloth shape and textures; this does not simulate a new drape or step physics. The user confirmed that the integrated fix follows and resets correctly when switching between a push-up and Salute, with world rate at zero. Broader cape classes and mission/respawn transitions remain unverified. Unsupported cape classes skip the correction without disabling body poses.
- Shader packs replacing the default shader registry require a registration merge. Run only one Photo Mode runtime loader at a time.
- Standalone ActionCam sharing supports the exact audited ActionCam 2.3.1 / runtime 10.31 and 2.4 / runtime 10.32 Lua contracts. Isolated tests cover both startup orders, gameplay profiles/FOV, binding levels, next/previous profile shortcuts, exclusive photo writes, First Person restoration and cleanup failures; live coexistence is not yet verified. War Journalist owns Photo Mode controls, replacing ActionCam's independent Photo actions. Unknown versions/hooks are left untouched and refused with a startup log message; disable that unsupported ActionCam and restart before using Photo Mode. Other camera mods require separate ownership integration.
- The registry-only repair passed tester-reported AMD Alt-Tab, fullscreen, bloom and DoF changes before and after Photo Mode opened. This is evidence for those reported reproductions, not a guarantee against every crash. HDR/SDR switching was not separately confirmed. Newly bundled logos, mission lights and every release cold-start combination have not all received fresh live acceptance.
